## Source-backed rebuild boundaries

- **Stealth jet:** KAN-946's selected subject remains a stealth jet. The cited USAF and Lockheed sources support limited F-117A context: single crew, two GE F404-F1D2 engines, internal stores, faceted exterior and radar-absorbent-material context. They do not establish that the chosen aircraft is an F-117A or support an exact service teardown. Do not copy protected imagery, diagrams, branding, or geometry.
- **CFM56-7B alternate example:** CFM sources support a schematic component order and selected stage/blade counts, not complete CAD dimensions or an actual disassembly path. Any newly modeled cutaway should be labeled schematic, not dimensionally exact or maintenance-grade. Do not copy brochure artwork.
- **Phone:** Choose the exact phone model before making model-specific claims. The cited Apple iPhone 15 repair manual supports names of serviceable modules and procedure headings, not permission to reproduce diagrams, artwork, industrial design, or marks.
- **Car engine:** The cited DOE source supports generic piston, cylinder, crankshaft, and four-stroke explanations, not the exact internals of the V6 candidate. Avoid variant-specific claims until a primary source for the selected variant is available.
