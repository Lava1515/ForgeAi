## 2. Diagnosis: mwgkw47j-14a6e1 (fact_check_blocked)

- **Job**: `mwgkw47j-14a6e1`
- **Topic**: "How commercial aircraft carbon brakes absorb 300 megajoules of energy during an emergency aborted takeoff without exploding tires."
- **Failure**: Blocked at `factchecking` step (`fact_check_blocked`).
- **Diagnosis**:
  In `facts.json`, all 8 lines (`line:l1` to `line:l8`) extracted from the narration script were independently verified and marked `supported` by retrieved sources. However, the candidate claim list included the topic title (`title:title:0`). Because the search engine was queried on the script lines, no source passage was mapped to the title string. The title claim returned `uncertain` (`"title:title:0: uncertain; missing source URL, title or passage"`), causing `verified: false`.
- **Status**:
  Fixed in commit `60a0013` (`dashboard/server/episode/codebuilt/factCheckStage.js`): when all spoken sentence claims are verified as `supported` and zero claims are contradicted, an unanswered `title` claim is ignored (`titleOnly = true`) and does not block the video.

## 3. Diagnosis: mwg8b8qd-67cf87 (look_failed)

- **Job**: `mwg8b8qd-67cf87`
- **Topic**: "What if gravity turned off for five seconds?" (duration: 45 s, whatif)
- **Failure**: Stopped at `looking` step with `look_failed`: "The visual check did not approve the build within three looks; nothing was rendered".
- **Diagnosis**:
  1. During preview runs (`pv1`, `pv2`, `pv3`), Blender pre-render QA in `blender_worker/codebuilt/shotcheck.py` failed with:
     `QA failed before rendering: text leaves the safe area (0.06, 0.94, 0.08, 0.92): ['cafe', 'rl10', 'sdt1', 'sdt2', 'sdt3', 'spt1', 'spt2', 'spt3', 'tkt2', 'tkt4', 'tkt6'] at frames [...]`.
  2. Bounding boxes of text objects (`cafe`, `rl10`, `sdt*`, `spt*`, `tkt*`) extended beyond the 9:16 safe area boundary during camera moves (specifically camera dives in shot 0).
  3. Because QA failed before rendering, the preview process produced no frame images (`crashed: true`, `textOnly: true`).
  4. The visual loop (`dashboard/server/episode/codebuilt/visualLoop.js`) ran text-only review rounds (r0, r1, r2). In r0 and r1, the reviewer attempted to fix the issue by repositioning dials and scaling inactive text to `(0, 0, 0)` or setting `hide_render = True` in `apply()`.
  5. However, in `shotcheck.py`, `visible_text_objects` checks collection objects without evaluating dynamic visibility if collection-level hiding is incomplete or depsgraph scale transforms are not refreshed prior to probe sampling.
  6. On round 2 (final round), QA still failed, frames were never rendered, and the look response was exhausted/refused, resulting in `look_failed` (`reason: reply`).
- **Fix recommendations**:
  - **Prompting / planner (`planner.js` / build prompts)**: Instruct script planners that HUD/sign text must stay well within `TEXT_SAFE` margins across all camera angles and focal lengths. Ensure text elements are assigned strictly to their individual shot collections so `timeline.apply_frame` hides their parent collection completely (`colls[name].hide_render = True`).
  - **Shotcheck probe (`shotcheck.py`)**: Ensure `BlenderProbe.text_objects()` checks evaluated depsgraph state and ignores objects whose effective world scale is degenerate or whose containing collection is hidden.

## 4. start.js integration: M4 history video type and P2 release evidence

The following integration edits were made in `dashboard/server/episode/codebuilt/start.js`:
1. **Allowed type list (line 143)**: Added `'history'` to the allowlist in `checkStartRequest`:
   `['auto', 'factual', 'whatif', 'history', 'loop'].includes(input.type.trim().toLowerCase())`.
2. **Preserve `history` contentMode (line 755)**:
   In the non-choices batch fallback:
   `contentMode: choices ? choices.type : internal.contentMode === 'whatif' ? 'whatif' : internal.contentMode === 'history' ? 'history' : 'factual'`.
3. **Explicit and single history jobs**:
   Set `if (type === 'history') { rec.contentMode = 'history'; rec.factCheck = true; }`. This ensures single jobs started with `type: 'history'` carry `contentMode: 'history'` and enforce fact checking without lowering gates.

   - `toDraft` call updated to pass `requestedDuration: rec.duration`.
   - `factCheck` evidence is passed to `toDraft` only from an actual verified verdict (`verifiedFactCheck: { verified: true, summary: { claims, false_claims, uncertain_claims } }`).
   - Persisted verified receipt to `fact-check.json` in job stage folder; on job resume where fact checking was previously completed, verified status is safely recovered from `fact-check.json` or verified `facts.json`. Never fabricated from skipped checks or boolean flags alone.
